CSGO Skins

CS:GO skins are cosmetic item designs used in Counter-Strike: Global Offensive that change the appearance of weapons without affecting gameplay.
They come in different rarities, finishes, and wear levels, which makes some skins extremely common and others very rare and expensive.
Players obtain them through in-game drops, cases, or trading, and many skins have become valuable collectibles with real-world market prices driven by demand, rarity, and aesthetics.
